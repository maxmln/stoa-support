# Stoa privacy policy

Last updated 30 September 2026.

Stoa is made by Max Bogdanoff. It has no account, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics, and it makes no network requests.

## What stays on your device

Your journal entries, the lessons and exercises you complete, and the quotes and stories you save are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. They are included in your iPhone backups like any other app data. Deleting an entry in the app deletes it. Nothing is sent to any server: all lessons, stories and quotes are bundled with the app.

## Notifications

If you turn on the morning or evening reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.
