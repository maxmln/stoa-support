# Stoa support

Questions, problems or ideas: email maxbogdanoff@gmail.com.

## Common questions

**Where do the quotes come from?** From public-domain English translations of the ancient texts (George Long, Richard Gummere, Aubrey Stewart and others), lightly modernised in places. Every quote shows its reference, such as Meditations 4.3 or Letter 13, so you can look it up in the original.

**What counts towards my streak?** Any day on which you write a journal entry, finish a lesson or mark an exercise as done. A day you haven't finished yet doesn't break the streak.

**How do I change or turn off the reminders?** Tap the gear icon at the top of Today, Learn, Stories or Journal and use the Reminders section. The morning reminder shows the day's quote; the evening one asks Seneca's review questions.

**Can I edit or delete a journal entry?** Tap an entry to edit it; the Delete button is at the bottom. In the Journal list you can also press and hold an entry to delete it.

**Can I start the lessons again?** Yes. Settings → Reset Lesson Progress clears finished lessons and exercises. Your journal and saved quotes stay.

**Is my journal backed up?** It lives only on your iPhone and is included in your regular iPhone or iCloud device backup. There is no account and no sync between devices.
